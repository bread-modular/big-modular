# Source / pin / analog review ledger

**Checked 2026-09-24.** These are concise extracted design facts, not redistributed datasheets. No stock quantities or prices are claimed. Product identifiers do not prove availability or pin-compatible substitution.

## Pin basis and settings

- **CH224K:** datasheet v1F figure 6.1: VDD1 via 1k and 1uF; CFG2=2, CFG3=3; DP4, DM5; CC2=6, CC1=7; VBUS sense8; CFG1=9; PG10; exposed ground is called 0 in WCH, mapped to 11 in the installed KiCad SSOP-10-EP footprint. CFG1=6.8k to GND requests 9 V; CFG2/3 float. Section 5.5 permits VBUS sense NC and requires DP/DM shorted together on-chip side, disconnected from connector, for PD-only use. Figure 6.1 shows no added Rd on CH224K; do not copy the adjacent CH224D external-Rd circuit. **The manufacturer's v1F text/figure does not specify a passive, unpowered CC Rd or guarantee 5 V attach before VDD is powered**; a powered reference schematic is not a dead-battery characterization. Qualify dead-battery/unpowered USB-C attach, PD 9 V, and non-PD 5 V on real hardware; generic fallback remains blocked. No guaranteed 9 V contract on a 5 V-only source; remain at 5 V if no 9 V PDO is agreed. R1, the 1k shunt-feed resistor, is specified >=0.5 W in a 2010 footprint pending exact part: at a 21 V fault and VDD>=2.4 V, a 1%-low resistor dissipates about 0.350 W; derate with temperature and check the actual CH224K VDD shunt budget. This 21 V steady-state estimate does **not** qualify a surge at a much higher VBUS. PG does not authorize load current. [source](https://components101.com/sites/default/files/component_datasheet/WCH_CH224K_ENG.pdf)
- **TPS26600:** IN1/2, UVLO3, NC4, OVP5, MODE6, SHDN7, RTN8, GND9, IMON10, ILIM11, dVdT12, NC13, FLT14, OUT15/16, EP=RTN. RTN must not be externally shorted to GND (TI section 9.3.5.5). 40.2k ILIM gives 0.2985 A **nominal only**: TI's ±5% is specified at 1 A, not this setting, and a 0.30 A input limit leaves narrow/unverified load, startup and source-headroom margins. UV 249k/100k and OV 806k/100k use 1.19 V *typical rising* thresholds: about **4.153 V / 10.781 V**, not earlier 4.188/10.872; thresholds/tolerances and 4.2 V minimum operating input still apply. RUN opens the UV divider feed, leaving UV low. SHDN is intentionally floating per internal pullup description. MODE tied RTN selects auto-retry current limiting; overload/thermal cycling is not a clean system shutdown. 100nF slew capacitor is a starting point; startup/inrush is unvalidated. **TI explicitly specifies back-to-back FET reverse-current blocking**, including unpowered IN=0, OUT=24 V leakage in section 7.5 (typical 11 uA) and reverse trip in 9.3; deleted redundant series diode to regain about 0.45 V of nominal fallback headroom. This is not a USB compliance/thermal qualification; test backfeed on power loss. [source](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tps2660.pdf)
- **TPS62160:** pins 1 PGND, 2 VIN, 3 EN, 4 AGND, 5 FB, 6 VOS, 7 SW, 8 PG, EP9. 0.8 V reference and 40.2k/10k give 4.016 V. 2.2uH/22uF is the datasheet baseline; retain correct effective capacitance. Internal compensation; PG pulls down the TPS65131 enable net until the intermediate supply rises. [source](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tps62160.pdf)
- **TPS65131:** pins follow table 5-1; native library common pins remain connected. INP1/24 are boost switch nodes, INN5/6 connect input, OUTN13/14 drive the inverting inductor. **Negative diode cathode at INV_SW, anode at PRE_N**. VNEG15 senses negative output, FBN16 connects divider from PRE_N to VREF17. Positive feedback 1.05M/100k; negative 1.15M/100k; VREF=1.213 V gives ±13.9495 V nominal. PSP9/PSN11 ground disables power-save. CP21 10nF and CN18 4.7nF to ground follow compensation recommendations; VREF 220nF. 6.8pF feedforwards are rounded starting values from section 8.2.2.6 (6.8/R and 7.5/R). L2/L3 4.7uH, requested Isat≥3A; exact part/core loss/maximum switch-current margin pending. External diodes require 40 V rather than blindly copying the 20 V example at a different output. VIN4 uses 100Ω/100nF analog filtering. BSW7 unused is explicitly permitted but allows positive-output prebias. [source](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tps65131.pdf)
- **TPS62125:** pins 1 GND, 2 VIN, 3 EN, 4 EN_hys (intentionally NC), 5 FB, 6 VOS, 7 SW, 8 PG, EP ground. 0.8 V FB; 71.5k/10k sets 6.52 V. Table 2 permits 10uH/22uF for output 5..10 V. This topology is valid at 13.95 V input, but its up-to-1MHz/PFM behavior is a known limitation relative to the strict switching-frequency preference. [source](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tps62125.pdf)
- **TPS7A4901 / TPS7A3001:** both OUT1, FB2, NC3, GND4, EN5, NR/SS6, **DNC7**, IN8, exposed pad ground. DNC is never grounded. Minimum output effective capacitance 2.2uF, proposed nominal 10uF/25V. 10nF NR/SS and feedback feedforward require startup verification. Positive VFB=1.185 V, negative VFB=-1.176 V; each has 600mV maximum dropout in its specified maximum-load test. Positive EN tied positive IN; negative EN tied negative IN is explicitly permitted. Diodes are oriented to conduct from positive output back to input, and from negative input towards negative output, respectively, under reverse differential. They are NOT output isolation diodes or precise OVP. [source](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tps7a49.pdf) [source](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tps7a30.pdf)

## Output wiring / orientation

J2 is an **off-board standard 2×8, 2.54 mm cable interface**, not a selected footprint. Standard signal pairs are:

| Pins | Net |
|---|---|
| 1,2 | -12 V / red-stripe end |
| 3,4,5,6,7,8 | 0 V |
| 9,10 | +12 V |
| 11,12 | +5 V |
| 13,14 | CV — NC here |
| 15,16 | Gate — NC here |

The 10-pin interface ends at pins 9/10 and therefore **does not carry +5 V**. J2 is a 16-pin design because +5 V was explicitly requested. At final harness selection mark pin-1 triangle, red stripe and -12 V; verify electrical continuity at the actual mating face. Keying is not a substitute for polarity verification; historical keyed reversals exist. Physical key-to-pin-1 orientation is **not signed off until the exact connector is selected**. [source](https://doepfer.de/a100_man/a100t_e.htm) [source](https://division-6.com/learn/eurorack-power/) [source](https://northcoastsynthesis.com/news/ribbon-cable-experience/)

## Input TVS / surge decision — explicit no-fit blocker

**D1 SMBJ12CA is removed from the schematic and BOM; it is NOT a DNP placeholder on a PCB.** Its 12 V standoff, 13.3–14.7 V breakdown at 1 mA and 19.9 V clamp at 30.2 A (10/1000 µs) make sustained 15/20 V unsafe before F1 can be assumed to open. TPS26600's OVP disconnects **OUT**, not its IN or the upstream TVS. F1 is only an unselected 500 mA / 32 V candidate, with no verified interrupt capacity, time-current curve or I²t coordination. A nominal 0.30 A eFuse limit is **downstream** and cannot limit TVS current. [source](https://datasheet.octopart.com/SMBJ12A-Littelfuse-datasheet-9813940.pdf) [source](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tps2660.pdf)

A same-family **Littelfuse SMBJ24CA** is a *screening example, not a selected replacement*: bidirectional, 24 V reverse standoff; maximum 1 µA leakage at the specified 24 V and 25°C test point; 26.7–29.5 V breakdown at 1 mA; **38.9 V maximum clamp only at 15.5 A** for the specified 10/1000 µs pulse. Its 600 W peak capability is non-repetitive at 10/1000 µs, 25°C and specified mounting copper, derates with temperature, and has no guaranteed energy/current coordination with the unspecified hot-plug/ESD surge. The 24 V standoff would avoid the known continuous 20/21 V conduction problem within that tested voltage range, but does not prove negligible high-temperature leakage or cover *unspecified* VBUS tolerance. At an unknown peak current or waveform, 38.9 V is **not an upper bound**. No exact LCSC/JLC stock/assembly identity or qualified SMBJ footprint was established; none is asserted in the BOM. [source](https://datasheet.octopart.com/SMBJ12A-Littelfuse-datasheet-9813940.pdf)

The GCT USB4105 drawing B4 rates the receptacle **48 V DC** and VBUS contacts **5 A collectively**; it does not rate an arbitrary ESD/hot-plug pulse. The proposed F1's **32 V** is a candidate steady/fault interrupt rating, not a surge-voltage or clearing-time guarantee. TPS26600 is **60 V recommended IN / 62 V absolute IN**, with **70 V/10 ms only at 25°C**, not unlimited surge immunity. The input MLCCs are 50 V nominal, and CH224K's R1/CC network has no surge qualification. A 38.9 V pulse clamp specified at one current is below some numerical limits but neither bounds actual unknown surges nor establishes fuse interruption and thermal safety. USB-C cable/source impedance, pulse waveform/repetition, temperature, grounded-shell return and layout parasitics must be specified and tested before any TVS or fuse is selected. [source](https://gct.co/files/drawings/usb4105.pdf) [source](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tps2660.pdf) [source](https://components101.com/sites/default/files/component_datasheet/WCH_CH224K_ENG.pdf)

**Conservative implementation now:** no shunt TVS on VBUS; retain C2's 100 nF / 50 V local eFuse bypass and eFuse OVP (~10.78 V typical) for downstream disconnect on steady overvoltage only. TI recommends ≥0.1 µF local input bypass; in some transient cases it recommends ≥1 µF to limit falling input slew to 20 V/µs, subject to effective DC-biased capacitance and actual source inductance. Neither that capacitor nor the eFuse proves connector ESD, high-energy hot-plug or >rating transient safety; **do not fabricate or connect to an unqualified source**. A later design must prove steady 5/9/15/20 V plus defined upper tolerance stays below TVS standoff at worst temperature; calculate clamping at a defined surge current and circuit inductance against *every* upstream/downstream absolute rating; check TVS pulse-energy/repetition, fuse I²t and interrupt voltage, selected MLCCs, CC/VDD stress and real JLC/LCSC sourcing; then validate on hardware. [source](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tps2660.pdf)

## Review blockers not hidden by clean ERC

**Immediate no-fabrication blockers:** D1 is gone, so VBUS has **no qualified ESD/transient clamp** (or proved connector/fuse/CC/eFuse surge coordination); four J1 copper-pad-to-locating-NPTH clearances fail the 0.25 mm board rule (actual 0.1944 mm); PCB has no power electronics or routes. Also unresolved: CH224K passive/unpowered CC attach; 5 V fallback headroom/droop and eFuse 0.30 A margin; analog loop stability with actual parts; all load combinations; ripple and LDO PSRR/headroom; start/stop/brownout rails; host/source current authorization; TVS/fuse/cable fault coordination; independent output OVP and all-rail shutdown; resistor/capacitor derating; magnetic saturation; total thermal rise; exact J2 and strain relief. The circuit is connected as a *proposal*, not approved electrical design.
