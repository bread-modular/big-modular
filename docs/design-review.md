# A1 electrical / source review — no fabrication approval

## PD-only interlock: implemented versus unproved

CH224K v1F describes pin 10 as **active-low open-drain Power Good, customizable**; it does not give a sufficiently explicit 9 V fixed-PDO/current-valid guarantee, startup truth table or PG timing/current authorization. CFG1=6.8k selects 9 V; CFG2/3 may float (internal pulldowns). VDD1 uses R1=1k / C1=1u; CC2=6, CC1=7; DP4/DM5 short together but not to USB data; VBUS sense8 NC is permitted in PD-only mode. WCH ground/EP0 maps to footprint pad11. Dead-battery attach, PG leakage/sink levels, loss-of-contract and reset behavior need hardware proof. [source](https://components101.com/sites/default/files/component_datasheet/WCH_CH224K_ENG.pdf)

**Implemented electrical AND:** PG asserted low -> R28=22k -> Q1 MMBT3906 PNP (1 base, 2 emitter, 3 collector). The schematic symbol pin numbers match the selected Diodes part's SOT-23 pinout and the assigned generic SOT-23 pad numbering; Q1 is **not placed** on the J1-only carrier. Emitter uses PD_VDD; R29=100k returns base to emitter. Q1 collector supplies PG_PERMIT -> JP1 RUN -> EF_ENABLE -> TPS26600 SHDN7. R30=10k returns SHDN to **RTN**, not GND. With Q1 unpowered/off or JP1 open, the specified SHDN pullup current at 0.4V (10uA) through R30 implies ~0.1V, below the minimum 0.55V falling threshold; this is a static check, not a startup guarantee. At PG near 0V, VDD=2.4..3.6V and an illustrative VBE=0.7V, R28 plus R3=100k pullup would ask PG to sink roughly **0.10..0.17mA** (nominal resistors); R29 diverts 17..29uA of R28 current. Without a specified startup truth table, PG **must be treated as potentially low** already at 5V VBUS and throughout a supply rise: it is **not** a 9V-contract detector. The UVLO window must block the eFuse at 5V regardless of PG. The WCH sheet identifies PG as active-low open drain but does not specify an applicable PG sink-voltage/leakage/timing guarantee here. Q1's sub-mA saturation, high-temperature off leakage, reverse-fault reference voltages and PG margins still need qualification; the 28 gate cases are structural calculations, not circuit simulation. [source](https://www.diodes.com/datasheet/download/MMBT3906.pdf) [source](https://components101.com/sites/default/files/component_datasheet/WCH_CH224K_ENG.pdf) [source](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tps2660.pdf)

TPS26600 SHDN is **low = OFF**, high >=1V = enabled; floating would enable and is no longer allowed here. Its recommended SHDN range is 0..4V; CH224K's 2.4..3.6V VDD operating bounds are compatible in normal forward-powered operation, subject to the untested gate margins. RTN is not externally shorted to GND. UVLO3 uses 562k/100k from VBUS_FUSED to RTN, completely independent of RUN. OVP5 uses 806k/100k. MODE6=RTN selects current-limit auto-retry; pin17 EP=RTN, 8 RTN, 9 GND, 1/2 IN, 15/16 OUT. ILIM11=40.2k to RTN; dVdT12=100n to RTN. NC4/13, IMON10 and FLT14 unused. [source](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tps2660.pdf)

`reports/pd-gate-check.json` enumerates resistor 1% extremes, UV/OV min/max thresholds and +/-100nA sense leakage. Nominal UV rise/fall is 7.8778/7.282 V, OV rise/fall 10.7814/9.966 V. The computed worst-case UV falling threshold exceeds 5.5V; the worst-case OV rising threshold remains below 15V. Therefore **5 V is excluded even if RUN is closed and PG stuck active**; 15/20V is excluded by OVP. Window qualification alone cannot identify PD messages. Near-window and brownout behavior needs dynamic testing; delay specifications are not zero-time guarantees. [source](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tps2660.pdf)

R31=10k bleeds VPROT so typical eFuse off leakage does not freely charge downstream bulk. TI's off-state 12uA source leakage is typical, not a guaranteed maximum at all operating points. No claim of exactly zero residual rail voltage or immediate discharge is made; precontract power-path isolation must be measured. No >5V converter bulk/rail generation is permitted before the contract. The upstream 100n eFuse bypass and CH224K 1u VDD decoupling remain powered for negotiation. Upon PG loss, the intended sequence is SHDN low -> eFuse opens -> VPROT/V4 decay -> U3 PG pulls U4 enables low; stored energy means rails do not vanish instantly.

**Unresolved fabrication blocker:** PG plus the window does not prove an explicit 9V fixed PDO at >=0.5A. A compliant PD-source assumption and an external analyzer are required even for proposed tests; this does not fulfill automatic current-contract validation. STUSB4500 was screened as a possible explicit-PDO alternative (POWER_OK2/3 and POWER_ONLY_ABOVE_5V), but has NOT been substituted without checking its pinout, NVM image, matching/current policy, startup and power-path sequencing. Do not silently wire a generic POWER_OK signal in place of this gate. No claim of cryptographic validation is made. [source](https://www.st.com/resource/en/datasheet/stusb4500.pdf)

## Retained conversion chain and pin checks

- TPS62160 U3: 1 PGND, 2 VIN, 3 EN, 4 AGND, 5 FB, 6 VOS, 7 SW, 8 PG, 9 EP. R9/R10 40.2k/10k sets 4.016V; L1 2.2uH/C6 22uF. PG with a V4 pullup controls both TPS65131 enables. The 4V intermediate protects TPS65131's 5.5V ceiling; it is no longer justified as a low-input operating mode. [source](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tps62160.pdf)
- TPS65131 U4: INP1/24 boost switches; INN5/6 input; OUTN13/14 inverter switch; VNEG15 senses PRE_N; FBN16 divides to VREF17, **not ground**. D2 cathode PRE_P/anode BOOST_SW; D3 cathode INV_SW/anode PRE_N. R12/R13=1.05M/100k and R14/R15=1.15M/100k give roughly +/-13.9495V. PSP9/PSN11 grounded; BSW7 unused permits positive prebias only once the gated V4 exists. CP21 10n, CN18 4.7n, VREF 220n, feedforwards 6.8p are starting values; VIN4 has 100R/100n filtering. Select actual 4.7uH inductors and 40V diodes; stability/saturation are unqualified. [source](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tps65131.pdf)
- TPS62125 U5: 1/9 GND, 2 VIN, 3 EN, **4 EN_hys NC**, 5 FB, 6 VOS, 7 SW, 8 PG NC. R17/R18 71.5k/10k sets 6.52V; L4 10uH/C19 22uF is the Table 2 starting combination. Up-to-1MHz/PFM remains an always-MHz limitation. [source](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tps62125.pdf)
- TPS7A4901 U6/U8 and TPS7A3001 U7: OUT1, FB2, NC3, GND4, EN5, NR/SS6, DNC7, IN8, EP9 GND. Negative IN/EN to PRE_N is intentional; DNC never grounded. VFB is +1.185/-1.176V, yielding +11.9922/-11.9952/+5.0007V nominal. At least 2.2uF effective output capacitance is needed. Proposed 10uF/25V MLCCs, 10nF NR/SS and feedforward require startup/overshoot tests. Bypass diodes address reverse differential/wrong polarity, NOT precision OVP. [source](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tps7a49.pdf) [source](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tps7a30.pdf)

Headroom screening only: `13.9495*0.95 - 0.25 - 12*1.025 = 0.702V` versus 0.600V maximum dropout; +5V screen is `6.52*0.97 - 0.20 - 5*1.025 = 0.999V`. These do not prove PSRR/transient regulation. About 40mA/rail means 1.16W output, ~2.26W converter input under assumed efficiencies and ~0.217W LDO heat. Total loss can exceed 1W; actual PCB copper/thermal design does not exist. The ~0.30A eFuse setting has narrow unqualified startup/load margin and is NOT guaranteed +/-5% (that TI accuracy is specified at 1A). No rated rail current is advertised.

## J2 / sourcing / surge

Off-board 16-pin output: 1/2=-12V (red stripe), 3..8=GND, 9/10=+12V, 11/12=+5V, 13..16 NC (CV/Gate). Exact harness/key orientation and connector are unselected. Never parallel outputs with another supply; continuity-test at the mating face. [source](https://doepfer.de/a100_man/a100t_e.htm)

**D1 is absent**, not DNP. A 12V-standoff TVS is unsafe on sustained 15/20V upstream VBUS; the eFuse OVP only opens its output. The previously screened SMBJ24CA is still NOT selected: a pulse-clamp value specified at one current cannot bound unspecified hot-plug/ESD energy. No qualified VBUS/CC ESD/surge network, fuse I²t/interrupt coordination or cable/source transient definition exists. F1 is an unselected 500mA/32V requirement, not USB authorization. Upstream resistors/MLCCs and J1 need coordinated pulse/rating checks. **No fabrication.** [source](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tps2660.pdf)

R1 remains >=0.5W / 2010: at 21V, VDD>=2.4V and 1%-low R, about 0.350W; this is not surge qualification. BOM's inherited LCSC mappings are identifier references only (CH224K C970725, TPS26600 C544399, TPS62160 C324077, TPS65131 C87663, TPS7A4901 C16430); stock/assembly eligibility is not verified. All other MPNs/passives/low-head screws need procurement review. KiCad-derived symbols/unchanged GCT footprint retain upstream library licensing; repository MIT does not replace it.

## Precedent / license distinction

Powerline USB-C is **3HP, 20V PD**, CERN-OHL-P v2; its published schematic shows PG10 NC and VBUS directly feeding regulator inputs. Its README has a regulator-name inconsistency, so it was not treated as a pin/part authority. The useful reference is front-entry power plus two M2 standoff support points; README mentions 5mm standoffs and 7.5mm in the build notes, not a verified universal dimension. Our right-angle interposers are newly dimensioned for this perpendicular carrier, not copied 3HP geometry. No repository was cloned; no third-party CAD, artwork or PDF is included. Non-PD Rakit/Nanas/Adafruit examples are NOT used as contract proof. [source](https://github.com/Andreas-Dorfner/Powerline-USB-C) [source](https://github.com/Andreas-Dorfner/Powerline-USB-C/blob/main/LICENSE.md)
