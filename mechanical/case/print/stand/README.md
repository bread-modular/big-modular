# Tiered stand: print set

Generated by `mechanical/case/tools/build_stand.sh` (`mechanical/case/tools/build_stand.py`). Each folder holds the
parts for one tier; `common/` holds the parts every tier shares. A 2-row stand is tier 1 + tier 2,
a 3-row stand adds tier 3. Every part fits the 256 mm bed (the tier 3 top bar lies across it
diagonally, already turned in its file), and the cheeks print outer face down with no supports.

The cheeks reach 40 mm (`RISE`) up each case's sides: enough to guide the case, well below
the module faces. Each row has a guide wall on both cheeks beside the case, with a ledge under
its bottom plate along the whole case depth (rows 2 and 3: 6 mm; row 1: 3 mm), on a solid web band
(no windows next to the ledge). Rows 2 and 3 also have a lip over the case's plate step (see below). The guide wall is
12 mm thick only in a 6 mm rail along its bottom (on the ledge) and top, in 5 mm ribs across it
(at most 40 mm apart) and in the lip; the bays between are pockets open to the inside, down to
the web. The rest of the cheek is a 4 mm web with lightening windows (12 mm rims); it is 12 mm
thick only at the splice plate seats, the stretcher channels and the feet. The outer face is flat
(the splice plates sit on it), so the cheeks print outer face down with no supports.

The 12 mm stays where it is needed: the M3x10 spacer sockets (10.2 mm deep + 1.5 mm floor) and
the 12 mm rubber feet set it, and the outer face has to stay flat.

## Cases: no screws, rows 2 and 3 slide in under a lip

The case bottom plate and its outer socket wall stand 3.15 mm past each side panel's outer face,
13 mm up from the plate underside. On rows 2 and 3 a lip on each guide band covers that step
(2.4 mm over it, 0.5 mm above it, 6 mm tall), so the case can only slide along its rails: it cannot lift off,
and cable pulls cannot pull it out.

- **Row 3:** the lip runs the whole case depth. Set the case on the ledges above the upper end of
  the rails and slide it down under the lips until it rests on the stop lug at the lower (front)
  end of each rail. Take it out the same way: slide it up and off the top. The tier 3 top bar can
  stay on: the case slides over its beam and past its ends (slide it 144 mm up, clear of the bar,
  before you lift it away).
- **Row 2:** take row 3 out first. The row 3 lugs and ledge sit 41 mm behind row 2's back end, so
  row 2's lip only covers the front 35 mm of the case:
  set the case on the ledges 36 mm up the rails (its front end just behind the lip ends), then
  slide it down under the lips onto the stop lugs. Out: slide it 36 mm up, lift it off.
- **Row 1:** sits on a thin 3 mm ledge at the foot of each tier 1 cheek (the same 18 mm inboard
  reach as the upper ledges), so its weight goes into the stand and loads the front feet against
  the rows above instead of resting on the desk. It sits between the stop lugs on tier 2 behind it
  and a low front lug on each tier 1 cheek (8 mm long, 10 mm above the plate underside, against the
  bottom plate and the outer half of the side panel end), so it cannot slide out to the front. No
  lip: lift it 11 mm over the front lugs and slide it in or out (the rows above can
  stay in).
- The rows 2 and 3 stop lugs are short (6 mm) and reach only under the lip, against the end of the
  plate step, so the row below can slide up past them when it goes in.
- The cases keep their own M3x6 bottom-plate bolts; nothing changes on the case.
- A 2-row stand uses the same tier 2 pieces, so its row 2 lip is also 35 mm.

| --- | --- | --- |

| `tier2/` (row 2 + rear leg) | `tier2-left`, `tier2-right`, 1x `common/stretcher`, 2x `common/splice-plate-along` (joint 1-2; 2x `common/splice-plate-adapter` instead with an older tier 1, see below) | 2x M3x8 + 2 spacers (stretcher), 8x M3x8 + 8 spacers (splices), 4 rubber feet |

Totals: 2 rows = 1 stretcher + 2 splice plates, 3 rows = 1 stretcher + 4 splice plates; 6 rubber
feet either way.

- **One screw size: M3x8 socket head** (ISO 4762), always into an M3x10 hex spacer (5.7 AF
  socket, 10.2 mm deep) superglued in the part, the same way as in the case:
  - splice plates: 6 mm plate with a 3.2 mm counterbore, 2.8 mm under the head, 5.0 mm into the
    spacer in the cheek;
  - stretcher: through the 12 mm channel floor with a 9 mm counterbore, 3 mm under the head,
    4.8 mm into the spacer in the stretcher end.
  The build checks every stack (>= 4 mm into the spacer, not past its end) and that each spacer
  socket has >= 1.5 mm of print round it and under it.
- Both joints take the same splice plate along the butt edge (32 x 34 mm, 2 + 2 holes, 14 x 17 mm
  hole pattern): the 1-2 joint is 43 mm tall (40 mm rise + the 3 mm row 1 ledge), but its plate keeps
  the lower 40 mm it had before the ledge (holes 13 and 27 mm over the desk), so tier 1 and tier 2
  printed before or after the ledge take it. (A joint under 36 mm would get a plate across it instead.)
- `common/splice-plate-adapter` (35 x 34 mm) is only for a tier 1 printed while the 1-2 plate had grown
  with the ledge (35 mm plate, its upper spacers at 30 mm instead of 27 mm): round holes on the lower
  pair (13 mm) and 3 mm slots on the upper pair (27-30 mm), so it joins that tier 1 to any tier 2. One
  part fits both sides (print 2, use them on joint 1-2 in place of the splice plates); its top stays
  1 mm under the 40 mm butt edge of a tier 2 printed before the ledge. Joint 2-3 keeps the splice plate.
- Screws are only for building the stand (splices, stretcher), never for the cases.

## Tier 3 top bar

The tier 3 cheeks only join the stand at their lower end (splice plates to tier 2), so their top
ends spring sideways. The top bar ties them across the top ends, with no screws and no change to
the tier 3 cheeks (it uses the shapes they already have): a 10 mm thick bar on both cheek end faces,
its beam 23.5 mm deep under the bottom plate level (0.5 mm under it), so row 3 slides past it.

- **Cap (each end):** the plate sits on the cheek end face; the outer leg lies on the flat outer
  face and a shoulder in the recess under the ledge bears on the 4 mm web, so the cheek cannot move
  out or in (0.25 mm a side); a lip lies over the cheek top. They reach 12 mm down the cheek.
- **Snap:** a 2 mm finger hangs from the lip between the cheek inner face and the case side panel
  (above the case lip, 1.85 mm from the side panel). Its barb (0.9 mm, 30° lead-in, 45° retention)
  rides over the 6 mm end rail and clicks into the last guide wall pocket under it (0.6 mm overlap,
  0.33 mm lift to the barb), so the bar cannot lift off by itself.
- **Fit:** push it straight down the rails onto both cheek ends until both fingers click. **Off:** pull
  it straight up (firm pull: the 45° barb lets go). It can stay on while row 3 goes in or out.
- **Print:** back face down (legs and finger up), diagonal on the bed (282.5 mm long). The barb's 45°
  face is the only overhang; the finger bends along the layers (0.98 % strain at the click, PLA is fine).
- The build checks it (`checks.top_bar`): no clash with the cheeks or cases, play out / in / down /
  up / across, the lift off clear with every case in (barbs aside), the finger's room to flex with the
  case pushed against the lip (0.5 mm), and row 3's loading path past it. Cutaways: `bar3` (along the
  case side through the barb: finger, barb, end rail, pocket) and `bar3x` (across it: outer leg,
  shoulder, top lip, finger).

## Stretcher

Each stretcher end sits in a box groove on the cheek inner face: a U channel 20.5 mm wide (0.25 mm
slip each side of the 20 mm stretcher) with 3 mm walls standing 3 mm proud of the inner face, on
the 12 mm full-thickness floor the screw goes through. The channel is open on one side (the build
picks the first of up / back / front with a free path, `checks.stretchers.channel_open`): build
both cheek stacks, slide the stretcher into both channels from the open side, then screw each end.
The walls stop it turning or shifting; the screw only pulls it onto the floor. The build checks
the slide-in path against the whole stand (cases out) and that the walls hold the stretcher
within 0.25 mm the other ways.

## Feet (3M rubber pads)

The stand stands on 6 self-adhesive (3M) rubber feet, 12 x 12 mm, 3 per side, each stuck on a
flat 12 x 16 mm plate on the cheek underside (full cheek thickness, 8 mm tall block over it, 2 mm
past the foot at each end): tier 1 at its front end, tier 2 just behind row 1 and at the back of
the rear leg. No screws or spacers in the feet. The cheek is back to 12 mm thick (the 15 mm cheek
was only needed for the M3 pads).

- Row 1 sits on the tier 1 ledges, so the feet lift it with the stand (its bottom plate clears the
  desk by the foot height + 3 mm) and its weight bears on the tier 1 front feet.
- The stand's centre of mass (cases 1.5 kg each) sits between the front and rear feet.
- The splice plates are symmetric, so the same part fits the left and right sides.

## Case fit (checked in the build)

The build places the real case (`mechanical/case/print/assembly-preview.step`: bottom plate, side panels,
rails) in every row, plus an M3 socket head (ISO 4762, 5.5 x 3) in each bottom plate counterbore
and on each rail screw outside the side panels, and reports `checks.case_fit`:

- Every row: no clash with any case, screw head or standoff; 0.5 mm to the stop lugs (row 1: front
  and back).
- Side play: row 1 1.0 mm each side; rows 2 and 3 0.75 mm (lip edge to side panel, the bottom
  plate edge stays 1.0 mm from the band).
- Every row: each ledge carries 16.6 mm of the bottom plate width over the full depth
  (22 cm² a side). Rows 2 and 3: the case lifts 0.5 mm before the lips hold it. The bottom plate
  screw heads sit 0.2 mm up in their 3.2 mm counterbores above the ledge: socket heads only (a button or pan head
  stands proud and lifts the case off the ledge).
- Loading path (`checks.loading`, against the whole stand with the other cases out): row 1 lifts
  11 mm over its front lugs (also with rows 2 and 3 in) and slides out to the front; row 2 slides 36 mm up and lifts off, row 3 slides out of the top past the top bar, all clear.
- Cutaway meshes per row (`row<k>`: across the front bolt line; `stop<k>`: through the stop lugs,
  rows 2+ along the lip; `front1`: row 1's front lug; `load2`: row 2 at its loading position; `stretcher2`: the stretcher end in its channel) go to `mechanical/case/research/stand/fit/`; render them with
  `blender -b --python mechanical/case/tools/render_stand.py -- mechanical/case/research/stand out.png row2` (`bar3` / `bar3x`: the top bar on a cheek end) (`feet` / `bottom`: the pads from below; `inside`: the left cheeks from inboard, pockets and windows).
