# imix research: circuit, decisions, open checks (S3/R3)

IMIX is a 6HP, 4-channel DC-coupled buffered mixer for Eurorack CV or audio. Each
channel has an input jack and a level pot on one row, and all four sum to one MIX
output at the bottom. The schematic (`hardware/imix.kicad_sch`, ERC 0) and the routed
2-layer carrier (DRC 0, parity equal) are generated by `tools/`. `jlcpcb/` holds the
shared exporter output (not supplier-accepted). Nothing has been bench-tested.

History: S1/R1 was a 4HP layout (knob column over a 2x2 jack grid). S2/R2 moved to
6HP with each pot beside its jack and the mix socket at the bottom, with the pots
identified as Thonk Tall Trimmers; its circuit was a plain pot-across-the-input
mixer. S3/R3 (this revision) follows the Bread Modular `imix` topology at the user's
request: an inverting op-amp buffer in front of every pot sets a fixed Eurorack input
impedance and drives the pot, with feedback caps and per-op-amp decoupling.

## 1. Brief

- 4 channels: one input jack plus one volume knob each, mixed to one output.
- Accepts Eurorack CV *or* audio, so the signal path is DC-coupled end to end.
- Uses the same pots as Bread Modular: Thonk Tall Trimmers
  (<https://www.thonk.co.uk/shop/ttpots/>) on the `Potentiometer_RV09` footprint,
  50k as on the Bread Modular `imix`.
- 6HP, each pot beside its jack, mix socket at the bottom; panels and structure as
  in `modules/midi`. Module name **IMIX**.
- As Bread Modular `imix`: a buffer op-amp in front of each pot for a proper
  (Eurorack) input impedance, plus caps for noise/stability. No mute, no LEDs, no
  per-channel gain above unity.

## 2. Bread Modular baseline (read-only)

Source: `~/data/projects/bread-modular/modules/imix` (2.0.0, `a960b19`), read from
its native netlist. It is a 3.3 V single-supply breadboard mixer (3 x MCP6002):

```
IN_n -- 22k -- U(-) ; 22k || 100p feedback ; U(+) = V_MID_n      (inverting buffer)
U out -- 100R -- RV pin 3 ; RV pin 1 = V_MID_n ; wiper -- 22k -- U3A(-)
U3A: 22k || 100p feedback, (+) = V_MID ; out -- 1k -- MIXOUT     (inverting summer)
1M input bias to V_MID_n, 10u per mid-rail, 100n per op-amp, 100u bulk
```

IMIX keeps that **topology** (inverting buffer -> 100R -> pot -> summing R ->
inverting summer -> 1k out; feedback caps; 100n at every op-amp) and adapts it to
Eurorack: ±12 V rails with ground as the reference (no `V_MID` network, no 1M bias
or mid-rail caps), and 100k input/feedback resistors so the input impedance is the
Eurorack-usual 100k (22k would load sources). Pot part and value are the same
(Thonk Tall Trimmer B50K on `Potentiometer_RV09`, benjiaomodular library,
CC BY-SA 4.0, byte-identical to the `opt/KiCadLibraries/benjiaomodular.pretty`
copy).

## 3. Circuit (as drawn)

```
Jn tip (IN_n) -- R(n) 100k -- INV_n (Ux -, Ux + = GND); R(n+4) 100k || C(n+8) 20p -> BUF_n (Ux out)
BUF_n -- R(n+8) 100R -- POT_n = RVn pin 3 (CW); RVn pin 1 (CCW) = GND
RVn wiper (WIPER_n) -- R(n+12) 100k --+-- SUM (U3A -, U3A + = GND)
                                       R17 100k || C13 20p -> MIX (U3A out)
MIX -- R18 1k -- J5 tip (OUT)
Jn TN -- GND (unpatched input grounded)        U3B: (+) = GND, out -> (-) (unused)
Buffers: ch1 U1B, ch2 U1A, ch3 U2B, ch4 U2A (one TL072 per channel pair, see 3.3)
```

### 3.1 Signal path

- **Input buffer:** each channel is a unity-gain inverting stage. Its input sits at
  a virtual ground, so the **input impedance is 100k at every knob position**
  (S2 varied 33k..50k with the knob). The op-amp inputs never leave 0 V, so the
  TL072's common-mode limits and phase reversal near the negative rail do not
  apply: inputs up to the full ±12 V swing are handled (the stage clips near
  ±10 V, section "Headroom").
- **Level:** the buffer drives the 50k pot from ~0 Ω through 100R (isolation, as
  Bread Modular). Full CW gives unity for that channel, full CCW 0. Two inversions
  (buffer, summer) make OUT = WIPER1 + WIPER2 + WIPER3 + WIPER4 with the input
  polarity.
- **Pot loading:** the wiper drives a 100k into the summer's virtual ground. The
  wiper's source impedance a(1-a)·50k (12.5k at mid-travel) gives about -1 dB at
  mid travel against an ideal linear law (0.444 instead of 0.5), and exactly 0 dB
  at full CW. Taper **B (linear)**: used for both CV and audio by design (fast rise, fine control near the top; preferred for performance).
- **Unpatched input:** the PJ301M-12 TN switch grounds the tip when no plug is in,
  so an unused channel adds 0 V and its 100k input can't pick up hum.
- **HF / noise:** 100k ∥ 20 pF in every stage (4 buffers + summer) gives an 80 kHz
  corner: stability margin and HF hash roll-off, not audible. 20 pF rather than
  22 pF because only the 20 pF Basic part passed the strict exact lookup (section
  5). Each op-amp has its own 100 nF per supply pin (C3..C8) next to the pin.
- **Headroom:** the rails are about ±11.7 V (Schottky drop). TL072 output swing is
  about ±10 V, so each buffer passes inputs up to about ±10 V and the sum clips at
  about ±10 V. Four full-level ±5 V audio signals at full CW will clip; turn the
  pots down. Unity gain by intent.
- **Output:** 1k series (short-circuit/cable/mis-patch protection). Output
  impedance 1k. R18 is a **1 W 2512** (UNI-ROYAL 25121WF1001T4E). With the MIX tip
  shorted to sleeve and a 10.5 V DC output it dissipates about 110 mW (11 %). The
  sized case is a hard -12 V source patched into MIX while U3 sits at +10.5 V:
  22.5 V across R18, 22.5 mA (below the TL072 short-circuit limit), about 0.51 W
  continuously (51 % of 1 W). The earlier 250 mW 1206 would have run at about 2x
  its rating there, and a 0.5 W 1206 at about 100 %. No Basic 1 W 1k was found, so
  R18 is the one Extended part (by request, 2026-10-19).
- **DC offset (CV use):** TL072CDT Vos ≤3 mV max per stage. Each buffer has noise
  gain 2 (≤6 mV at BUF, scaled by its pot); the summer's worst-case noise gain is
  about 5 (all pots full CW). Worst case about 15 + 4 x 6 ≈ 40 mV at OUT if every
  offset adds with the same sign; typically a few mV. Recorded as bench item E2.
- **Spare:** U3B is unused, tied as a follower with (+) on GND so it idles at 0 V.

### 3.2 Power

- J10: 2x5 Eurorack header (pin 1 = -12 V at the board bottom, same convention and
  16-pin IDC envelope as midi). +5 V and the 16-pin extras are unused.
- D1/D2 B5819W in series with +12 V / -12 V: reverse-polarity protection (a
  reversed cable blocks both rails). C1/C2 10 µF bulk after the diodes; 100 nF at
  every op-amp supply pin: C3/C4 (U1), C5/C6 (U2), C7/C8 (U3).
- Current: 3 x TL072, ≤2.5 mA/amplifier max (about 1.4 mA typical), plus four pots
  (≤0.2 mA each) and the output load (≤1.1 mA into 10k): ≤17 mA per rail worst
  case, about 10 mA typical. Diode dissipation is negligible.
- PWR_FLAGs: `#FLG01` GND entry, `#FLG02` +12V and `#FLG03` -12V (driven only
  through D1/D2, which ERC can't see through). No other flags. The rails use stock
  `GND`/`+12V`/`-12V` power symbols (global nets).

### 3.3 Placement (docs/geometry.json)

- 6HP panel 30.0 x 128.5 mm (Bread Modular `Panel_6HP`), one rail hole pair at
  X7.5 as midi. Carrier 29 x 110 mm (X0.5..29.5, Y9.25..119.25). Panel unchanged
  from R2.
- Four channel rows at Y19/40/61/82 (21 mm pitch): input jack at X7.0, pot shaft
  at X21.0, channel number on the panel at X14 between them. MIX jack at X7.0/Y105
  with the `MIX` label to its right. `IN` / `LEVEL` column labels under the title.
- Pot: Song Huei R0904N drawing (Thonk `R0904N_Thonk.pdf`): 9.7 mm wide body
  6.8 mm tall, 1 mm collar, 6 mm insulated shaft to 25 mm above the PCB, no
  bushing; snap-in tabs; pins 2.5 mm pitch. The panel seats on the jacks (Z10.0),
  so the shaft stands 13.4 mm proud of the FR4 panel and 13.0 mm of the printed
  one. Thonk's Tall Trimmer Toppers (7.8 mm wide) need at least 8 mm, ideally 12.
- Knob room: 14 mm from pot shaft to jack centre, 21 mm between rows.
- All SMD parts on B. **Buffer clusters:** U1 at (16, 32) between rows 1 and 2,
  U2 at (16, 74) between rows 3 and 4. The left pin column (section B) is the upper
  channel, the right column (section A) the lower channel; each section has its
  feedback R ∥ C beside the column, its input R below, its 100R beside it, and its
  100 nF right at the supply pin. Each 100k summing resistor sits under its pot,
  above the wiper pin. U3 (summer) and R17/C13/C7/C8/R18 sit below RV4, left of
  J10; D1/C1 and D2/C2 sit to the right of J10.
- J10 vertical at the bottom right (columns X19.0/21.54, pin 1 at Y116.5). The
  16-pin IDC envelope (X16.47..24.07, Y94.91..120.31) covers only low SMD parts
  (U3 edge, C8, D1 edge, all below 2.5 mm); no THT tails are under it.

### 3.4 Routing (R3, tools/route_plan.py)

- Inputs: the upper channel of each pair runs on F from its jack tip down X10.5
  to a via, then on B down X9.0 to its input R. The lower channel runs on F from
  its jack tip across to a via under its input R. The high-impedance INV nodes stay
  inside each cluster (a few mm, B).
- Pot drive: upper channel 100R -> via -> F under its pot's pin row to pin 3;
  lower channel 100R -> B down X23.7 between the next pot's brackets to pin 3.
- SUM: wiper -> 100k (B) -> via -> F to the right-edge bus X28.3 -> down to
  Y91.5 -> via -> C13/R17 -> U3 pin 2. The bus sits on F at the board edge, away
  from the input lanes, between GND pours.
- U3: MIX -> R18 -> J5 tip; R18 stands vertically left of U3 between the J4 sleeve
  and J5 tip pads, MIX pad up (direct from R17/C13), OUT pad down to the J5 tip.
  U3B pins 6-7 shorted.
- Rails: B at the header/diodes/bulk caps. +12 V: F at Y103.05 to U3 and on to the
  left edge, then B up X3.2. -12 V: B along the bottom edge Y118.4 and up X1.6; F
  up X26.0 to U3. Each cluster takes F taps (+12 V at row+11, -12 V at row+14.2) to
  vias under the SOIC body.
- GND poured on both layers, a via at every SMD GND pad (op-amp (+) pins share a
  via under the body), stitched in every row. Tracks 0.25 mm signal, 0.4 mm
  power; vias 0.6/0.3 mm (64 vias).

## 4. Open checks

| ID | Check | State |
|---|---|---|
| M1 | Pot footprint and stack | **Open.** The Bread Modular RV09 footprint has its bracket slots 9.6 mm apart and the pin row 7.5 mm from the tab line; Thonk's R0904N drawing dimensions 10.6 mm and 7 mm (which edge each dimension is taken from isn't clear on the scan). Thonk calls the parts drop-in replacements for the common vertical trimmers, and Bread Modular uses them on this footprint, but **seat a real pot in a printed 1:1 copper plot (or a bare board) before assembly**. The 3D model is a proxy from the drawing (`tools/build_pot_model.py`; collar diameter assumed 7.0 mm). |
| M2 | Knobs | Open. Thonk Tall Trimmer Toppers (7.8 mm) fit easily; other 6 mm knobs up to about 12 mm clear the jack nuts and the channel number. |
| E1 | Bench: gain law, unity at full CW, clipping level, crosstalk, noise | Open (nothing bench-tested). |
| E2 | Output DC offset (CV) | Open. About ≤40 mV worst case by datasheet, typically a few mV (section 3.1). |
| F1 | Order gates | **Export done** (`jlcpcb/README.md`); supplier gates open: bottom-side Economic option, placement preview, review attestations, live stock recheck, DFM. |

## 5. Sourcing (docs/sourcing-candidates.json)

Live strict audit, 2026-10-19T17:00:35Z (`assembly.py audit --strict-live --boards 5
--side bottom`, `docs/jlcpcb-audit-requirements.json`). Every assembled part is
economy-eligible and JLCPCB **Basic** except R18 (Extended, see below), and every
code, MPN, package, stock and voltage/power/dielectric requirement passes:

| Refs | Part | LCSC |
|---|---|---|
| U1, U2, U3 | TL072CDT (ST), SO-8 | C6961 |
| R1-R8, R13-R17 | 100k 1 % 0603 UNI-ROYAL 0603WAF1003T5E | C25803 |
| R9-R12 | 100R 1 % 0603 UNI-ROYAL 0603WAF1000T5E | C22775 |
| R18 | 1k 1 % 2512 (1 W) UNI-ROYAL 25121WF1001T4E, **Extended** | C54315 |
| C9-C13 | 20 pF C0G 50 V 0603 CL10C200JB8NNNC | C1648 |
| C3-C8 | 100 nF X7R 50 V 0603 CC0603KRX7R9BB104 | C14663 |
| C1, C2 | 10 µF X5R 25 V 0805 CL21A106KAYNNNE | C15850 |
| D1, D2 | B5819W SOD-123 (CJ) | C8598 |

36 assembled parts in 8 BOM lines. The audit has one hard failure, on purpose:
R18 fails the shared "R/C requires actual Basic" policy, because no Basic 1 W 1k
was found (the same trade-off as 8bit R12 and midi V2 R20). The rule is not weakened;
expect an Extended-part fee on the order. R18's requirement is the -12 V mis-patch
(22.5 V, 22.5 mA, 0.511 W worst case at -1 %) with its package stated as `2512`,
because the shared footprint matcher only knows 0402-1210. Its power, voltage and
current checks pass. The audit also reports gates it treats as **unknown**, all
left open on purpose:
- the human review attestations (land pattern, pin mapping, ratings; and the role
  for each capacitor) for every part;
- the capacitor ESR/ESL/DC-bias limits;
- the package/footprint review for D1, D2 and U1-U3, which aren't standard chip
  footprints.

C1653 (22 pF) shows as Basic in search, but its exact strict lookup returned
missing/ambiguous/truncated, so it isn't used. Hand-soldered, with no LCSC field
(`assembly: manual` in the requirements): J1-J5 PJ301M-12, RV1-RV4 Thonk Tall
Trimmer B50K, J10 2x5 header. A catalog snapshot is not an order or stock
reservation.
