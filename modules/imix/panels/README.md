# imix panels (6HP)

| Directory | Contents |
|---|---|
| `front-pcb/` | Native passive FR4 panel `imix-panel.kicad_{pro,pcb}`: 30.0 x 128.5 x 1.6 mm, NPTH only, F.SilkS labels. Generated by `../tools/build_layout.py` from `../docs/geometry.json` |
| `front-cad/` | FreeCAD printable panel, 2.0 mm thick, labels engraved 0.4 mm: `build_front_panel.py`, `.FCStd`, `.step`, `.stl` |

The two panels are alternatives with the same holes: 2 rail holes Ø3.2, 5 jack
holes Ø6.2 and 4 pot holes Ø7.2 (Bread Modular `PanelHole_Potentiometer_RV09`; 0.6 mm radial
clearance on the 6 mm Tall Trimmer shaft). Either one mounts on the jack bushings
with the PJ301M-12 nuts, plus the two rail screws. The snap-in pots have no bushing
or nut; their shafts stand about 13 mm proud for Thonk Tall Trimmer Toppers
(open check M1 in `../docs/research.md`).
Regenerate the printed panel headless from `modules/imix`:

```sh
~/Applications/FreeCAD_1.1.3.AppImage freecadcmd panels/front-cad/build_front_panel.py
```

The script reads the outline, NPTH holes and labels from
`front-pcb/imix-panel.kicad_pcb` and asserts them against `docs/geometry.json`. It
checks for a single valid solid, the drilled-blank volume, a non-zero engraving and a
closed manifold STL. Holes are nominal: ream after printing. The geometry is nominal
only; no physical-fit or strength claim.
