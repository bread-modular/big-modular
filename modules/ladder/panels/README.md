# ladder panels (10HP)

| Directory | Contents |
|---|---|
| `front-pcb/` | Native passive FR4 panel `ladder-panel.kicad_{pro,pcb}`: 50.5 x 128.5 x 1.6 mm, NPTH only, F.SilkS labels. Generated by `../tools/build_layout.py` from `../docs/geometry.json` |
| `front-cad/` | FreeCAD printable panel, 2.0 mm thick, plain (no labels): `build_front_panel.py`, `.FCStd`, `.step`, `.stl` |

The two panels are alternatives with the same holes (jacks Ø6.2, pots Ø7.2, REC LED Ø3.2, REC button Ø4.2, rail holes Ø3.2). Either
one mounts on the jack bushings with the PJ301M-12 nuts plus the rail screws; the RV09
pots take no nut. The REC button needs a printed cap through its hole (the modules/8bit cap). Regenerate the printed panel headless from `modules/ladder`:

```sh
~/Applications/FreeCAD_1.1.3.AppImage freecadcmd panels/front-cad/build_front_panel.py
```

The script reads the outline and holes from `front-pcb/ladder-panel.kicad_pcb` and
asserts them against `docs/geometry.json` (as `modules/midi`). It checks for a single
valid solid, the drilled-blank volume (plain, no engraving) and a closed manifold STL.
Print suggestion: PETG/ABS, front face down, 0.2 mm layers. Holes are nominal: ream
after printing. The PJ301M-12 threads leave ~0.5 mm past a 2.0 mm nut on the 2.0 mm
print; LED domes sit 0.4 mm behind its front. Not test-printed; no physical-fit claim.
