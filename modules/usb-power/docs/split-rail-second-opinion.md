## Positive raw setpoint

## Negative: Basic N12 + NRAW <= 15 V with 1 V separation?

**No.** Basic final divider gives |N12| up to 12.76–12.77 V; low impedance does not help (9.1k+120/1k: 12.763 V). The needed NRAW then peaks 15.23–15.47 V even with a precision raw divider; any Basic raw divider peaks 15.7–16.5 V. Root cause is TPS65131 VFBN ±24 mV x ~12.7 plus VREF/resistor spread, not impedance.

- Precision final (90.9k 0.1% + 1.2k Basic / 10k 0.1%, |N12| 11.612–12.386 V) + precision raw:
  - RB 150k: ±50 nA peak 14.857 V (feasible); ±100 nA 15.040 V (fails by 40 mV).
  - RB 121k (10.0 uA VREF load = spec condition): ±50 nA 14.821 V; ±100 nA **14.969 V** (31 mV margin; ratio window only ~0.025, so standard-value realization must be checked exactly).
- Prior 046 candidate (R14 1.6M 0.1%, R60 180k Basic, R15 150k 0.1%, R76 2.4k; R72 90.9k 0.1%, R83 0R, R73 1.2k, R74 10k 0.1%): headroom 1.015 V / peak 14.932 V at ±50 nA; 0.926 V / 15.022 V at ±100 nA. Acceptable as a disclosed exception: 1 V accuracy separation conditional on typical IFBN; worst case still > 0.8 V max dropout (do not claim ±2.5% there). Material fix vs accepted 0.178 V baseline.
- Reject very low-impedance raw dividers (e.g. RB 15k): ~80 uA VREF load vs 10 uA spec condition — reference accuracy uncharacterized.
- Optional user decision: N12 nominal ~-11.95 V adds ~50 mV margin.

## Capacitors / C62

- C62 DNP for first prototype: supported (CFF not required); costs PSRR/noise, removes FB kick path. Keep pads.
- N12 output bank (3x C12891) screens 11.40 uF vs 10 uF; 9.51 uF with AC-level sensitivity. Add a 4th C12891 (Basic) if placement allows. Negative input 12.36 uF is also thin.
- PRAW boost output caps: size on bias-derated capacitance at ~14 V for ripple well under 0.1 V peak; >= 25 V rating (consider 35/50 V per TI example for bias derating).

## Must-fix / blocking checklist for Astra

1. **SYNC** tied directly to AGND.
2. **FREQ**: RFREQ to AGND; ~80k (460–740 kHz) or ~120k (~400 kHz typ); TI recommends ~350 kHz nominal for foldback recovery. Note foldback below 280 kHz may not recover with load attached.
3. **EN**: internal 950k pull-down; drive from VPROT via divider (abs max 34 V) or eFuse PG. EN low does not disconnect: PRAW ≈ VPROT - Vd whenever VPROT exists, so the positive LDO can see ~8 V. Gate TPS7A4700 EN so it releases only after boost regulation.
4. **SS**: 47–100 nF (≈14–30 ms typ). Does not limit passive L+D inrush into raw caps at eFuse turn-on; eFuse dV/dt must cover it.
5. **COMP**: §8.2.1.2.4 with bias-derated Cout and Gea min 240 uS; fc <= min(fRHP/5, fsw/10). At 1 A raw, 10 uH, D≈0.42: fRHP ≈ 72 kHz, so fc <= ~14 kHz. Check light-load DCM and 0→target steps.
6. **Inductor/diode**: Isat > 7.75 A (TI guidance), RMS >= ~2.6 A; Schottky >= 3 A avg, VR >= 30–40 V, must survive continuous eFuse limit (<=2.13 A) into a PRAW short (EN cannot open the pass path).
7. **FB open/OV**: open RSH drives max duty; no OVP verified in reviewed sections. Robust FB placement; consider a clamp vs 25 V caps / LDO.
8. **Thermal**: TPS55340 PowerPAD to AGND (AGND/PGND tied at one point) with via array to inner GND; LDO EPs likewise; no clip-on heatsink; JEDEC theta is not board theta.
9. **TPS65131**: ENP held at GND independently of ENN; retain positive L/D/C/FB/comp on an isolated unloaded node (precharged ~MID - Vd), never routed to a connector; U4 VIN/INN only from MID — never 9 V; common ground.
10. Front-side placement, inductor height vs brackets, no other mechanical change.

Testable limits (B): PRAW 13.15–13.89 V DC, >= 13.0 V at TPS7A4700 IN incl. ripple; |NRAW| <= 14.9 V DC / 15.0 V peak; |NRAW|-|N12| >= 1.0 V typical, >= 0.8 V worst; source <= 1.85 A at +400/-200/+5 250 mA; LDO Tj estimate <= 125C at 50C ambient.
