# vca-mix panels (6HP)

| Directory | Contents |
|---|---|
| `front-pcb/` | Native passive FR4 panel `vca-mix-panel.kicad_{pro,pcb}`: 30.0 x 128.5 x 1.6 mm, NPTH only, F.SilkS labels. Generated by `../tools/build_layout.py` from `../docs/geometry.json` |
| `front-cad/` | FreeCAD printable panel, 2.0 mm thick, plain (no labels): `build_front_panel.py`, `.FCStd`, `.step`, `.stl` |

The two panels are alternatives with the same holes: 10 jacks Ø6.2, 3 pots Ø7.2
and 2 rail holes Ø3.2. The pots are Song Huei R0904N tall trimmers (plain Ø6.0
knurled shaft, no thread), so either panel mounts with the 10 jack nuts plus the
two rail screws; the pots take no nut. Regenerate the printed panel headless from `modules/vca-mix`:

```sh
~/Applications/FreeCAD_1.1.3.AppImage freecadcmd panels/front-cad/build_front_panel.py
```

The script reads the outline and holes from `front-pcb/vca-mix-panel.kicad_pcb`
and asserts them against `docs/geometry.json`. Print suggestion: PETG/ABS, front
face down, 0.2 mm layers. Holes are nominal: ream after printing. Pot stack
(datasheet nominal, `docs/research.md` D3): the shaft tip stands 13.4 mm (FR4) /
13.0 mm (printed) proud of the panel and its 7 mm knurl sits wholly above it. No
physical-fit claim.
